About Kedar Farmhouse

Kedar Farmhouse is a private farmhouse in Niveli, Angaon, Bhiwandi, made for families, friends and groups. Come for a day or stay the night, with open green space, a swimming pool, slides and swings, and a kitchen and dining area, all to yourselves.

It's an easy escape from the city, with quiet mornings, room to play and no one else on the property.

  • Private Only your group on the property
  • Open space Lawns, games and seating
  • Day or night Picnics and overnight stays
